## 2026-09-22 — Module 1: Muscle, Bone & Joints — why muscle actually grows

**What I learned:** Muscle doesn't grow in the gym — it grows during recovery.
Training causes microscopic tears in the fibre; the growth happens afterward
when the body repairs it slightly thicker, using protein and sleep to do it.
Two things drive it: progressive overload (the muscle only grows if the
demand keeps increasing) and DOMS (the soreness two days later is repair,
not damage — and not something to chase for its own sake).
